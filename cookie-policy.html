Cookie Policy

How we use cookies to improve your experience.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to the owners of the site.

2. How We Use Cookies

ViierMods uses cookies for several essential purposes: to keep you logged into your account securely, to save your site preferences (such as dark mode and NSFW filters), and to track basic analytics so we can improve platform performance.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies: Required for the operation of the site, such as authentication and security cookies.
Functional Cookies: Used to recognize you when you return and remember your choices.
Analytical/Performance Cookies: Allow us to count the number of visitors and see how visitors move around our website.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control and/or delete cookies as you wish through your browser settings. However, if you choose to disable essential cookies, certain features of ViierMods, such as logging in or downloading age-restricted mods, will not function correctly.
