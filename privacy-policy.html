Privacy Policy

Learn about how we handle your data.

1. Information We Collect

When you register an account on ViierMods, we collect information that identifies you, such as your email address, username, and IP address. We also collect data regarding your usage of the platform, including downloaded mods, search history, and forum activity.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to provide and improve our services, communicate with you about your account, process premium subscriptions, and prevent fraud or abuse on our platform. Your download history helps us generate personalized recommendations and maintain download statistics for mod authors.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share anonymous, aggregated data with partners for analytics. In cases of legal requests or to enforce our Terms of Service, we may disclose your information to the necessary authorities.

4. Your Rights

Under GDPR and CCPA, you have the right to request access to the data we have stored about you, request corrections, or request the deletion of your account and associated data. You can manage these preferences from your account dashboard.
